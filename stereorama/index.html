Stereorama360° 3D VR Camera

Turn around once with your iPhone and get a 360° stereo panorama you can look around in VR.

Panoramas are stitched entirely on your iPhone and kept in the in-app gallery as Cardboard Camera .vr.jpg files, with optional ambient sound. Turn on Save to Photos to keep each one in your photo library too. View them by dragging, by moving your phone, or in VR mode with a Cardboard-style viewer. One-time purchase: no subscription, no ads, no account, nothing uploaded.

7. Safe use and VR viewing

Please read this section before capturing panoramas or using the Cardboard VR viewing mode.

While capturing

  • Capturing requires you to turn around in place while looking at your screen. Make sure you have enough space, stand on stable ground, and stay aware of your surroundings, traffic, edges, stairs and other people.
  • Do not capture while driving, cycling or operating machinery, or in any situation where it may be unsafe.

VR viewing

  • Do not use VR mode while walking, driving, riding or otherwise moving, or in any situation that requires your attention. A VR viewer blocks your view of your real surroundings. Sit or stand in a safe, open space, clear of obstacles.
  • Take regular breaks, for example at least every 10–15 minutes, even if you feel fine.
  • Some people experience discomfort, motion sickness, nausea, dizziness, disorientation, eye strain, headaches or blurred vision when viewing VR content. If you experience any of these, or any other discomfort, stop immediately and rest until the symptoms have passed. Do not drive or do anything demanding until you have fully recovered.
  • If you are prone to seizures, have had a seizure or epilepsy, are pregnant, elderly, have a heart condition, binocular vision problems or another serious medical condition, consult a doctor before using VR mode.
  • Children are more sensitive to these effects. VR mode should be used by children only under adult supervision, for short periods.
  • When using a Cardboard or other VR viewer, follow the viewer manufacturer’s instructions and safety warnings. We do not make or supply viewers and are not responsible for them.

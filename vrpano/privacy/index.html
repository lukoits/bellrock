Privacy Policy — VRPano

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

  • VRPano captures 360° stereo panoramas with your iPhone’s camera, stitches them entirely on your device, and saves them as .vr.jpg files (the Cardboard Camera format) to your Photos library. It also keeps a gallery of your panoramas inside the app and lets you view them, including in a Cardboard VR mode.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not run any servers, and the app does not upload your panoramas, recordings or any other data anywhere.
  • The app contains no analytics, no trackers, no ads and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share a panorama through the iOS share sheet, or you email us.
  • The app is free and has no in-app purchases.

Permissions and what they are used for

iOS asks for your permission before the app can use each of the following. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security or in Settings → Apps → VRPano.

Permission What the app uses it for Where the result goes If you decline
Camera Capturing the images that are stitched into a panorama. Processed on your device into the panorama; not sent anywhere. You cannot capture new panoramas. You can still view panoramas already in the app.
Microphone (optional) Recording a short ambient sound during capture, which is embedded in the panorama file and played back in the viewer. Stored inside the panorama file on your device and in your Photos library; not sent anywhere. Panoramas are captured without sound.
Motion & Fitness (motion sensors) Tracking how far you have turned while capturing, so the images can be aligned and stitched. Used on your device during capture and stitching, and kept with the capture recording in the app’s storage; not sent anywhere. Capture cannot track your turn and may not work.
Photos — add only Saving finished panoramas to your Photos library. Your Photos library. Panoramas stay only in the app’s gallery.
Photos — full or limited access Filing saved panoramas into a “VRPano” album, and importing VR panoramas that are already in your Photos library into the app’s gallery. The app reads only the photos needed for these features, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Panoramas can still be saved (with add-only access), but are not filed into the album, and you cannot import from your library.

The app does not ask for any other permissions. If you allow only limited Photos access, the app can see only the photos you selected.

Information stored on your device

The following information is kept in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. We do not receive it.

  • Panoramas and capture recordings: the panoramas in the app’s gallery, including any ambient sound you recorded, and the capture recordings (camera frames and motion sensor readings) the app uses to stitch a panorama.
  • App settings: your preferences inside the app.

Panoramas you save to Photos are stored in your Photos library and are managed by iOS. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs them to your iCloud account under your agreement with Apple; we have no access to it.

Device backups. The app’s files may be included in iCloud Backup or a computer backup, depending on your device settings. Such backups are handled by Apple or by you, and we have no access to them.

Deleting data. You can delete panoramas inside the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored in the app’s private storage from your device. Panoramas you saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them in the Photos app. Copies in device backups, in iCloud Photos, or that you have shared with others are not affected. Because nothing is stored on our side, we cannot restore deleted panoramas.

Sharing panoramas

If you choose to share a panorama, the app opens the iOS share sheet. The panorama file goes only to the app, service or person you pick there, under that recipient’s own terms and privacy policy. This is entirely your action, and we do not receive a copy.

A .vr.jpg file contains the images of both eyes and, if you recorded it, the ambient sound. Please keep this in mind before sharing a panorama of private places or people.

Support emails

The Support row in the app’s Settings opens your email app with a new message to us. If you send it, we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything you attach. We use this only to answer you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying to your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or steps taken at your request. Your email is delivered by your own email provider and stored in our mailbox, which is provided by Google (Gmail).

Apple

The app is distributed by Apple (through the App Store or TestFlight), and Apple processes information about your download under its own terms and privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you have chosen in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements to share data with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics that do not identify you. If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may also share with us the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send through TestFlight, together with the name and email address associated with your TestFlight invitation. These are features of iOS and Apple’s services, not of the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use an account, sign-in or any server of ours.
  • It does not upload panoramas, sound recordings, photos or sensor data to the cloud.
  • It does not contain analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution or social-network SDKs, or any other third-party SDKs.
  • It does not show ads and does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • It does not sell or share personal data.

How long information is kept

  • Panoramas and app data: on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app.
  • Panoramas saved to Photos: in your Photos library until you delete them there.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and up to 2 years afterwards, unless a longer period is required by law or to establish or defend legal claims.

Your rights

Because the app keeps your panoramas and settings only on your device, you control that data directly: you can view, delete or share it at any time, and deleting the app removes it from the app’s storage. We hold no personal data about you, except the emails you send us.

Depending on where you live, and in particular under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you (in practice, your emails), to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, email us (see Who we are and how to contact us). We will respond within one month, which may be extended where permitted by law.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please write to us first — we will try to sort it out. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If a child has emailed us and you would like that email deleted, contact us.

California and other US states

The app does not collect personal information, and we do not sell or share personal information (including for cross-context behavioral advertising). If you email us, we receive the identifiers and content described in Support emails and use them only to reply. You have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email us.

Security

Your panoramas and app data are kept in the app’s private storage on your iPhone, which iOS protects with its app sandbox and, when your device is locked with a passcode, data protection encryption. Because the app sends nothing over the network, there is no transmission of your data to secure. No method of storage is completely secure, so please protect your device with a passcode.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top, and we will highlight material changes on this page and, where appropriate, in the app. If a future version of the app starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
